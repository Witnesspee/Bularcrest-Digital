## Brand & Style

This design system embodies bespoke high-end modernism, tailored for ultra-high-net-worth individuals, architectural collectors, and institutional patrons. The aesthetic establishes a quiet, atmospheric authority through deep space, precision optics, and sculptural materials.

The visual direction merges **Minimalist Precision** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**. Dark obsidian expanses are illuminated by diffuse celestial orbs of warm champagne gold and abyssal blue. The interface evokes the feeling of viewing private architectural models in an exclusive twilight gallery: refined, monolithic, and impeccably curated.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the deep obsidian baseline `#0A0A0A`, providing infinite contrast without absolute harshness. 

- **Primary (`#C5A059`)**: Muted metallic gold representing craft, warm illumination, and architectural prestige. Used sparingly for critical focal points, interactive strokes, active states, and refined micro-accents.
- **Secondary (`#1B2A4A`)**: A nocturnal deep marine blue, applied within ambient gradient washes and glass surface backdrops to bring architectural depth and spatial resonance.
- **Tertiary (`#8C7A5B`)**: Antiqued bronze utilized for secondary labels, low-intensity dividers, and hover tints.
- **Neutral (`#0A0A0A`)**: Grounding carbon foundation, scaled through semi-translucent stepped layers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` through `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) to form structural surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast classicism of **Playfair Display** with the engineered neutrality of **Inter**.

- **Playfair Display**: Reserved strictly for architectural narrative headlines and editorial moments. Headings must always maintain relaxed, breathable line-heights and slight negative tracking on large scales to retain sculptural tension.
- **Inter**: Serves all technical specifications, body copy, and navigation. Body copy leans on light weights (`300`) with generous leading to convey modern editorial serenity.
- **Micro-labels (`label-caps`)**: Always presented in uppercase with extensive kerning (`0.18em`) to mimic architectural blueprint legends.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column grid system bounded by a max-width container of `1440px`. 

Spatial composition relies on extensive, deliberate voids:
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column framework with `2rem` gutters and generous `5rem` page margins. Sections breathe with macro spacing (`space-xl` / `6rem` or larger) separating key conceptual phases.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` margins.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` canvas margins. Stacked structural cards transform into full-bleed horizontal scrolls or linear monoliths.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque stacking or drop shadows. Instead, it is expressed through luminous physical layers and atmospheric depth:

1. **Environmental Underlay**: Deep spatial backdrops feature static or slowly floating blur spheres (`filter: blur(120px)`) utilizing `#C5A059` at 12% opacity and `#1B2A4A` at 25% opacity against `#0A0A0A`.
2. **Surface Glassmorphism**: Cards and panels utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` combined with an ultra-thin border stroke (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)` on interactive focus).
3. **Ambient Occlusion**: Floating cards project ultra-diffused, soft dark-tinted shadows: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06) inset`.
4. **Interactive Bloom**: Hover states invoke a micro radial gradient glow originating from the cursor point or button center, rendering glass surfaces tactile and responsive.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft** geometry (`roundedness: 1`), calibrated to mirror contemporary monolithic architecture:

- Base panels, modals, and cards use `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corner curvature, asserting clean structural lines without sharp industrial brutality.
- Action items (buttons, pill indicators) adopt controlled architectural radii rather than complete capsules, maintaining geometric dignity.
- Circular elements are strictly reserved for media preview framing, focal aperture rings, or orbital ambient light sources.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Obsidian background bounded by a sharp `1px` border of metallic gold (`#C5A059`). Text is set in `label-caps` in `#C5A059`. On hover, the background transitions to `#C5A059` with `#0A0A0A` text, accompanied by a subtle `0 0 24px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)` halo.
- **Ghost Action**: Completely transparent fill with a muted border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), shifting to full white typography on hover with a crisp bottom underline stroke.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- Monolithic structures featuring `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and background of `rgba(15, 15, 15, 0.65)`.
- Bounded by a `1px` hairline stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Architectural photography inside cards maintains a subdued grade, shifting from an ambient duotone contrast to full natural color on card hover.

### Inputs & Inquiry Fields
- Borderless base surface with a persistent bottom line of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Subtle glass fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`).
- On focus, the bottom border shifts to `#C5A059` accompanied by a gentle horizontal gradient reveal. Placeholder text remains set in `Inter` Light with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`.

### Chips & Meta Badges
- Miniature glass tiles with `0.25rem` radius, `padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Rendered with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` fill, uppercase `label-caps` typography, and hairline gold or neutral borders denoting project categories (e.g., "RESIDENTIAL", "MONUMENTAL", "COMPLETED").

### Architectural Stat Indicators
- Minimalist data presentation blocks: large numerical values in `headline-lg` paired with micro descriptive metadata below in `label-caps` with `#8C7A5B` coloring.
